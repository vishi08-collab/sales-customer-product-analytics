# Sales, Customer & Product Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product revenue, and monthly sales trends. It provides a consolidated view of business KPIs to help stakeholders evaluate performance and identify growth opportunities.

## Dashboard Preview

![Sales Analysis Dashboard](Sales%20Analysis%20Dashboard%20Infographic.png)

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 5K |
| Total Revenue | $22M |
| Total Customers | 3K |
| Total Products | 2K |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | 46% |

*Values are based on the dashboard visualization.*

## Dashboard Features
- Country-wise revenue analysis
- Customer segmentation and order analysis
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Monthly revenue and order count trends
- Product distribution by gender
- Year-wise monthly revenue comparison
- Country-level revenue contribution analysis
- Business insights and recommendations

## Key Business Insights
- The USA is the highest-revenue country in the displayed analysis.
- Returning customers account for 46% of orders, highlighting the importance of customer retention.
- Product A is the leading product by revenue in the displayed comparison.
- Monthly revenue trends highlight changes in sales performance over time.
- Country and product comparisons help identify opportunities for growth and diversification.
